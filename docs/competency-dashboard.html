04 Framework Provenance

Domains follow the five-module structure of the 2009 ACCP/SRLF consensus statement on competence in critical care ultrasonography (basic echocardiography, lung, pleural, abdominal, vascular), which ACGME program requirements effective July 2024 now mandate for CCM and PCCM fellows, plus procedural guidance.

Echo items and gates follow the LUCC Delphi consensus (CHEST 2022): five standard 2-D windows, qualitative interpretation targets, a minimum of ~30 mentored scans, and a longitudinal arc of introductory training → mentored formative portfolio → summative acquisition assessment → final cognitive assessment. Scan-count targets for the other domains (25 proctored exams each) follow common practice in published fellowship curricula (ATS Scholar 2024 best-practices statement).

Scale is an entrustment scale: a fellow is signed off on a domain when all its items reach ≥3 and the scan target is met.
